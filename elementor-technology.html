The X5 Foot Scanner

The X5 Foot Scanner is more than a foot scanner — it is an AI-based biomechanical assessment and customised insole system. It assesses foot structure, lower-limb alignment and overall body posture, then helps generate analysis reports and customise insoles on-site. The manufacturer states that the process takes approximately 10 minutes.
