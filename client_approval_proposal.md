## 📐 Page 1: Advanced Technology & Equipment

### 🎯 Primary Objective
Position Wellness Pro as a leader in scientific foot biomechanics and non-invasive pain relief by demystifying the technology behind the custom fitting process.

### 🧩 Section Breakdown & Copy Blueprint

#### 1. Hero Section
* **Eyebrow Badge:** 🔬 Precision Healthcare & Biomechanical Engineering
* **Heading:** Next-Generation Diagnostic & Recovery Technology
* **Subheading:** Experience how Wellness Pro combines European medical standards, 3D foot scanning, and custom thermal molding to deliver instant pain relief and optimal alignment.
* **CTAs:**
  - `[Primary]`: Schedule a 3D Foot Scan
  - `[Secondary]`: Watch Technology in Action (Video Anchor)

#### 2. Key Technology Pillars (3 Column Interactive Showcase)
* **Pillar 1: FootBalance 3D Digital Foot Scanner**
  - *Mechanism:* High-precision optical sensors mapping arch height, foot pronation, supination, and weight distribution.
  - *Clinical Benefit:* Identifies kinetic chain imbalances that cause knee, hip, and back strain.
* **Pillar 2: Custom Insole Moulding Station (100% Custom in 10 Min)**
  - *Mechanism:* Rapid thermal activation pillows that mold the insole directly to the patient's foot contour under dynamic standing load.
  - *Clinical Benefit:* Individualized support customized to both left and right feet independently.
* **Pillar 3: Kineon Move+ Laser & LED Targeted Joint Therapy**
  - *Mechanism:* Dual-wavelength targeted photobiomodulation (deep red & near-infrared laser).
  - *Clinical Benefit:* Stimulates blood flow, accelerates cartilage repair, and provides targeted joint pain relief.

#### 3. Machine Demonstration Video & Photo Showcase
* **Source Assets:** WhatsApp Video clips & high-res equipment photos from Drive Folder 1.
* **Layout:** Interactive media gallery with video overlay cards showing:
  - Video 1: Dynamic foot analysis & sensor live view
  - Video 2: Step-by-step custom insole moulding demonstration
  - Video 3: Device ergonomics & clinical sanitization standards

#### 4. The 4-Step Patient Journey (Timeline)
1. **Step 01 - Computerized Scan:** Comprehensive foot arch and pressure mapping.
2. **Step 02 - Biomechanical Consultation:** Specialist evaluates gait and alignment.
3. **Step 03 - Precision Thermal Moulding:** Insole warmed and customized in 10 minutes.
4. **Step 04 - Instant Fit & Follow-up:** Walk out with immediate support and lasting relief.

#### 5. Conversion Banner
* **Heading:** Ready to experience medical-grade foot comfort?
* **Action:** Book your complimentary consultation at the nearest Wellness Pro center.
